# Remote Work Procedure

## 1. Principle
Remote work is open to any role whose activity does not require permanent
physical presence. It rests on a written agreement between the employee and
their manager.

## 2. Number of days allowed
Remote work is limited to 3 days per week. Teams in direct contact with clients
are limited to 2 days per week. A temporary exemption may be granted on medical
grounds upon presentation of a certificate.

## 3. Equipment and allowances
The company provides a laptop and a headset. A flat allowance of 20 EUR per
month covers the internet connection. Furniture purchases are not covered.

## 4. Information security
Internal documents must never be stored on personal equipment. Access to the
information system is exclusively through the company VPN. Any security incident
must be reported within 24 hours.

## 5. Reversibility
Either party may terminate the remote work agreement with one month's notice.
The initial adjustment period is 2 months.
