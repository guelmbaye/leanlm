# Leave Policy

## 1. Paid leave
Each employee accrues 2.08 days of paid leave per month worked, that is 25
working days per year. The reference period runs from 1 June to 31 May.

## 2. Request deadlines
A leave request must be filed at least 15 days before the departure date for an
absence shorter than 5 days, and 30 days before for a longer absence. The
manager has 7 days to respond.

## 3. Exceptional leave
Exceptional leave covers marriage (4 days), the birth of a child (3 days), the
death of a close relative (3 days) and moving house (1 day). A request for
exceptional leave is approved by the head of human resources, after review by
the line manager.

## 4. Carry-over and balance
Unused days may be carried over up to a limit of 5 days, until 31 December of
the following year. Beyond that, they are forfeited.
