# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope
This policy applies to all employees of the company, including interns and
contractors on long-term assignment. It defines the rules governing the
reimbursement of business expenses incurred on the company's behalf.

## 2. Submission and reimbursement deadlines
Every expense report must be submitted within 15 calendar days of the expense.
Approved expense reports are reimbursed within 30 working days from the date of
approval by the line manager. A report submitted after the 15-day deadline may
be rejected without appeal.

## 3. Applicable ceilings
The following ceilings apply per person and per day:

| Category | Ceiling | Receipt |
|---|---|---|
| Lunch | 25 EUR | required |
| Dinner | 35 EUR | required |
| Accommodation | 120 EUR | required |
| Urban transport | actual cost | required |

Exceeding a ceiling requires a written justification and prior approval from the
finance director.

## 4. Non-reimbursable expenses
The following are never reimbursed: traffic fines, personal expenses, alcohol
purchased outside client entertainment, airline upgrades, and any expense
claimed without an original receipt.

## 5. Approval chain
Approval happens in two stages: the line manager confirms business relevance,
then the accounting department checks documentary compliance. In case of
disagreement, the finance director arbitrates.
